# Arbortec Forestwear

Innovative chainsaw protective apparel, footwear, and accessories engineered for arborists, forestry professionals, and outdoor specialists.

---

## About Arbortec

For more than 30 years, Arbortec has been designing high-performance forestry PPE and technical apparel that combines protection, comfort, and durability in real-world working environments.

We develop products for:
- Arborists
- Forestry professionals
- Utility workers
- Climbers
- Outdoor specialists
